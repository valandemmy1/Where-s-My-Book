How it works

A story beyond the story.

No account is required for a finder to add a stop.

01

Register

Add the title, author, and starting city. The site creates a unique Book ID.

02

Print

Print the small “Where's My Book?” plate with its QR code and place it inside the book.

03

Pass it on

The next reader scans, adds their city and a note, then sends the book onward.
